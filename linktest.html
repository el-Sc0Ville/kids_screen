How to run it (about 10 minutes)

  1. Open this page on one of the kids' iPads, signed in to your streaming apps.
  2. Tap each yellow button. When you come back here, tap what happened.
  3. Then tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen", open the new icon, and run the list again. It keeps both runs separately.
  4. Tap "Copy results" and paste them to Claude.

Some "App link" buttons may show a "Cannot open page" message. That's a result too: tap ❌.
