Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Twrlzy helps parents choose what their kids watch: a short list of shows you approved, opened in the right streaming app for the time you set. This policy explains what the Twrlzy app for iPad and iPhone keeps, where it goes, and how you control it.

The short version

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Twrlzy never sells or rents information, and never shares it for marketing.
  • Kids never type anything: they tap pictures. A parent enters each kid's first name (a nickname works too), age and picture.
  • Everything stays on your device unless you turn on Connect devices or alerts.
  • You can delete all of it, anytime, from inside the app.
  • Twrlzy doesn't include any streaming subscription: you sign up and pay for each streaming app yourself.

Who we are

Twrlzy is made by Patrice Normandin, in Québec, Canada. For any question about privacy, or to ask us to delete information, write to lesc0vill3+twrlzy@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Twrlzy keeps these on the iPad or iPhone it runs on:

  • Each kid's first name or nickname, age, and picture (a drawing, an emoji or a letter, never a photo).
  • Your rules: daily show time, approved shows and grades, your streaming apps, snacks and the look.
  • Each kid's day and History: the shows they picked, and about how long each one played.
  • Your Parental controls PIN, saved in a scrambled form, not as the digits.
  • Your Screen Time choices.

Screen Time. Twrlzy uses Apple's Screen Time to lock and unlock streaming apps. Apple keeps the names of your apps private, even from Twrlzy, which only gets Apple's coded tokens for them. Twrlzy can't see what plays inside a streaming app. Apple tells it about how long a show's app was used, and that stays on your device.

Face ID. When Twrlzy asks you to confirm it's you, your iPhone or iPad checks Face ID, Touch ID or its passcode. Twrlzy only learns yes or no. Your face, fingerprint and passcode never leave Apple's secure hardware.

If you connect devices (optional)

Connect devices lets your phone and your kids' iPads share one set of rules. If you turn it on:

  • Your family's shared rules (the kids' first names, ages and pictures, show time, apps, approved shows, snacks, the look, and the PIN in its scrambled form) and each kid's day are kept in your family's private space on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), in Montréal, Canada. So is a Forgot your PIN? request, until it's answered.
  • Each device signs in anonymously: a random ID, with no name, email or password.
  • Only the devices in your family, up to 6, can read or change your family's space.
  • Each device's language, who uses it, and its Screen Time lock stay on that device.
  • Delete our family, in Connect devices, removes your family's space from the server, for every device.

If you turn on alerts (optional)

Alerts tell your phone when a kid starts a show. If you turn them on:

  • Your phone's alert address (a push token) is kept in your family's space.
  • Each alert carries a kid's first name, the show and the time it ends. It travels through Expo's push service, then Apple's (Apple Push Notification service), to your phone.
  • Requests to approve a new PIN (Forgot your PIN?) travel the same way.
  • When you turn alerts off, your phone's token is removed.

Show pictures and where to watch

  • To show each show's picture and check where shows stream in your country, Twrlzy asks The Movie Database (TMDB) through Twrlzy's own small helper server, which runs on Cloudflare. For shows on Tubi, it also checks Tubi's own web page. Show pictures load from TMDB's image server.
  • These requests name shows and a country, never anything about your kids. When you add a show by its title, that title is searched on TMDB the same way.
  • Twrlzy's helper doesn't log or store requests. It only counts the requests from each internet address for a few seconds, to stop misuse. Cloudflare, which runs it, handles network traffic as its own privacy policy describes.
  • Where-to-watch data comes from JustWatch, through TMDB.

YouTube videos

Shows from a show's own official YouTube channel can play inside Twrlzy, in YouTube's embedded player in its privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube, which Google runs, operates that player and handles information as Google's Privacy Policy describes. You can turn this off in Parental controls > Streaming apps: those shows then open in the YouTube app.

Streaming apps and subscriptions

Twrlzy doesn't come with any streaming subscription. Installing Twrlzy, or paying for it, doesn't give you access to Netflix, Disney+ or any other streaming app: you sign up for each one, and pay for it, yourself. Twrlzy recommends shows from many apps, but it only offers your kids shows on the apps you turn on, and a show on an app you don't subscribe to won't play.

Twrlzy opens shows in those apps. It doesn't sign in to them or see your accounts there, and each one has its own privacy policy.

App updates

Twrlzy gets small updates through Expo's update service (EAS Update). Each update check sends technical details, such as the app's version, the kind of device, and a random install ID that Expo uses to deliver updates.

What Twrlzy doesn't do

  • No ads, and no analytics or tracking tools from anyone else.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites. Twrlzy doesn't use your device's advertising identifier.
  • No accounts with an email or password.
  • No chat, and no way for kids to share anything with anyone.

Children's privacy

Twrlzy is made for parents to set up and use with their kids. Kids don't create accounts or enter information: a parent does, in Parental controls, which only opens with a 2-second hold and the parent's PIN. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents can see, change or delete everything Twrlzy keeps about their kids, right in the app, or ask us at lesc0vill3+twrlzy@gmail.com.

How long information is kept

  • On your device: until you change it or delete Twrlzy.
  • In your family's space: until you tap Delete our family.
  • Alert tokens: until you turn alerts off, or that phone stops sharing.

Your choices

  • Turn alerts off: Parental controls > Alerts.
  • Stop a device sharing with your others: Parental controls > Connect devices > Stop sharing.
  • Delete your family's shared information, for every device: Parental controls > Connect devices > Delete our family.
  • Delete everything on a device: delete Twrlzy like any app. That also removes its Screen Time lock.
  • Ask us what we hold about your family, or to correct or delete it: lesc0vill3+twrlzy@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

Where information is processed

Your family's space is stored in Montréal, Canada. Some services Twrlzy relies on work from other countries, including the United States: Expo and Apple for alerts and updates, Cloudflare for the helper, and TMDB for show information.

Security

Twrlzy's connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Your family's space can only be opened by your own devices. The PIN is saved in a scrambled form, never as the digits.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. If a change affects what Twrlzy keeps or shares, the app will say so before it applies.
