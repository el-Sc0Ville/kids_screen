Quick answers

What is Twrlzy?

Twrlzy turns "what can I watch?" into a calm daily routine. Your kid gets a surprise theme and picks 1 of 3 shows you approved. Twrlzy opens it in the right streaming app, for the time you set.

Does Twrlzy include Netflix, Disney+ or other apps?

No. Twrlzy doesn't come with any streaming subscription: you sign up for each app, and pay for it, yourself. Turn on only the apps you have, in Parental controls > Streaming apps. Kids only get shows on those, and a show on an app you don't subscribe to won't play.

A show didn't open, or won't play
  • Open that streaming app on the same device and make sure you're signed in, with your kid's profile chosen.
  • Check that you still subscribe to that app.
  • Shows sometimes leave an app. Twrlzy checks once a day and moves them under Taking a break, in Parental controls > Shows.
  • Your kid can come back to Twrlzy within 2 minutes of Let's watch and pick again, once: the time comes back too.
How do I set up the Screen Time lock?

On your kid's iPad, open Parental controls > Screen Time and follow its 5 steps. Apple asks for the iPad's passcode, Face ID or Touch ID. Then Apple's list opens for each streaming app: search for the app and tick it, and the next one opens by itself. With the lock on, a show opens only its own app, for the time you set.

I forgot my PIN
  • Tap Forgot your PIN? under the PIN pad.
  • If your phone is connected, with alerts on, it gets a request: open Twrlzy there, tap Approve and confirm with Face ID, Touch ID or the phone's passcode. Then choose a new PIN on the device that asked.
  • On your phone itself, Face ID is enough.
  • With no phone connected, Twrlzy asks the grown-up number instead, as in the first setup.
How do I connect my phone and the kids' iPads?

On the first device, open Parental controls > Connect devices and tap Start: a code shows right away. On each other device, type that code in Join with a code (on a new device, it's on the first screen). The devices then share the same rules. Each one keeps its own language and Screen Time lock.

How do I get alerts on my phone?
  1. Get Twrlzy on your phone.
  2. Connect it to your family (see above).
  3. On the phone, open Parental controls > Alerts and turn alerts on.

When a kid starts a show, your phone shows who, what and until when.

How do I turn Twrlzy off, or delete our information?
  • To stop the lock: Parental controls > Screen Time, turn off Lock streaming apps.
  • To stop one device sharing with the others: Parental controls > Connect devices > Stop sharing.
  • To remove your family's shared information for every device: Parental controls > Connect devices > Delete our family.
  • To remove Twrlzy from a device: delete it like any app. That removes its lock too.
Does Twrlzy show ads?

No. Twrlzy has no ads and no analytics. Free streaming apps, and plans with ads, still show their own ads.
